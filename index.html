How to get MANLY

Open the camera or choose up to 10 photos from your gallery. Each photo detects up to 8 faces. Use the photo arrows to move through your selection; your edits are kept for this visit. Pick a plaster. In Photo mode, tap the shutter to freeze and fine-tune, then tap Save photo beside the preview for a direct download. In Video mode, record a new clip or use Upload to choose an existing video. For an uploaded clip, choose a plaster and tap Create filtered video. The preview is a test frame; this button creates the complete downloadable clip. Keep this tab open until the video ends; processing runs in real time. Tap the shutter to stop early and save the processed part. When finished, Download video appears directly beside the preview; no confirmation dialog is required.

Your face stays on your device.

Photos, camera frames, audio, and face landmarks are processed in your browser. MANLYFY does not upload them. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or subscriptions. Your browser downloads the app and face-tracking model from this website. Your photo count, unlock progress, design, display preferences and up to 200 small visual rating fingerprints may be stored locally. Rating fingerprints contain no image or face landmarks and group similar pictures on this browser; they are never uploaded. Clearing site data resets progress. Media is never saved in your progress. Manliness scores are fictional pixel-based game results, not judgements about gender, identity, appearance or personality. Real plasters already present in a photo are indicated with the checkbox; digital ones are counted automatically.

Camera & microphone

The camera needs HTTPS and browser permission. The microphone is optional and off by default; turn it on in Video mode before recording. Denying the mic still allows silent video. Camera and mic stop when you leave the tab or turn the camera off.

Nose not cooperating?

Face forward in even light, with your whole face visible. Photos automatically find up to 8 faces. Turn on Live group for up to 4 people on camera; single-face mode is faster. Small, covered, dark, or strongly sideways faces may be missed. For photos, add and drag a plaster manually. For slower phones choose Lite mode.

Opening your photos

Choose JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or another format supported by your browser. Select up to 10 photos, at most 35 MB each and 100 MB per set. Photos open one at a time to protect phone memory. If a HEIC/HEIF photo will not open, export a JPG/PNG copy from your gallery. Only the active photo is decoded; selected files and edits stay in this tab.

Uploaded videos

Choose one clip up to 3 minutes and 150 MB, in a format your browser can decode (usually H.264 MP4 or WebM). Preview, seek and choose your plaster before processing. Original audio is kept when enabled and supported; the app does not request your microphone for an imported clip. Processing and recording happen locally in real time. The optional 3-second MANLYFY ending adds an animated logo and “IT’S MANLY NOW.” after the clip; turn it off before creating a video for an ending-free export. Small or obscured faces may be missed.

Downloads & sharing

Photos download as watermark-free PNG files, up to 2560 pixels. 9:16 and square formats fit your whole photo with a dark border. Videos record at up to 1280 pixels and download as WebM or MP4 according to your browser. Some social apps need a format conversion. Native file sharing appears where supported; otherwise download and share from your gallery.

Install & offline

On Android Chrome, tap Install app or use the browser menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen. After the tracker has loaded once online, previously loaded app and tracking files can be reused offline. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted.

A few honest limits

Recording requires MediaRecorder and canvas capture support. Record with the tab visible; switching away stops the recording. Clips stop at 3 minutes to protect phone memory. Tracking is a playful 2D effect, not a perfect 3D wrap. Browsers without WebGL2 use a local CPU worker. Motion tracking updates between neural scans; fast turns, covered faces, and leaving the frame can interrupt it. First face acquisition can take longer on slower devices. Real Android devices and low-powered hardware still need hands-on testing.
