How to get MANLY

Open the camera or choose up to 10 photos from your gallery. Each photo detects up to 8 faces. Use the photo arrows to move through your selection; your edits are kept for this visit. Pick a plaster. In Photo mode, tap the shutter to freeze and fine-tune, then tap Save photo beside the preview for a direct download. In Video mode, record a new clip or use Upload to choose an existing video. Choose a plaster before uploading a clip. Uploaded videos process automatically using your chosen plaster. When finished, tap the video to play it and use the large DOWNLOAD VIDEO button directly underneath. To change designs, tap Edit designs & create again, choose the faces and plasters, then Process video & get download. Precise export checks every source frame and can take longer than the clip. Keep this tab open until it finishes. Choose Compatibility to process during playback instead. No confirmation dialog is required to download.

Your face stays on your device.

Photos, camera frames, audio, and face landmarks are processed in your browser. MANLYFY does not upload them. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or subscriptions. Your browser downloads the app and face-tracking model from this website. Your photo count, unlock progress, design, display preferences, creator access when activated, and up to 200 small visual rating fingerprints may be stored locally. Rating fingerprints contain no image or face landmarks and group similar pictures on this browser; they are never uploaded. Clearing site data resets progress. Media is never saved in your progress. Manliness scores are fictional pixel-based game results, not judgements about gender, identity, appearance or personality. Real plasters already present in a photo are indicated with the checkbox; digital ones are counted automatically.

Camera & microphone

The camera needs HTTPS and browser permission. The microphone is optional and off by default; turn it on in Video mode before recording. Denying the mic still allows silent video. Camera and mic stop when you leave the tab or turn the camera off.

Nose not cooperating?

Face forward in even light, with your whole face visible. Photos automatically find up to 8 faces. Turn on Group camera for up to 8 people on camera. Uploaded videos always detect up to 8 faces, with a separate design for each tracked face. Choose a face in Style your faces, then pick its design. Single-face camera mode is faster. Small, covered, dark, or strongly sideways faces may be missed. For photos, add and drag a plaster manually. For slower phones choose Lite mode.

Opening your photos

Screenshots are welcome. Use Gallery for pictures or Choose files to browse downloaded images and PDFs without a file-type filter. JPG/JPEG/JFIF, PNG/APNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, SVG and ICO use browser decoding with local recovery paths. HEIC/HEIF and TIFF have local compatibility converters; unusual encodings can still fail. Animated images use a still frame. PDFs open their first page; password-protected PDFs, Word documents, ZIPs and camera RAW files are unsupported. Select up to 10 files, at most 35 MB each and 100 MB per set. Images open one at a time, up to 80 megapixels with native decoding (resized to at most 32 megapixels for editing) or 32 megapixels through compatibility conversion. If a cloud-gallery file is unreadable, download it to this device and use Choose files. Ranking needs no face detection. Selected files stay in this tab and are never uploaded.

Uploaded videos

Choose one clip up to 3 minutes and 150 MB, in a format your browser can decode (usually H.264 MP4 or WebM). Choose a plaster before uploading. Processing starts automatically, with a visible 0–100% indicator. Tap the finished video to play it; DOWNLOAD VIDEO is directly below it. Use Edit designs & create again to preview, seek or give each face a different plaster before processing again. Original audio is kept when enabled and supported; the app does not request your microphone for an imported clip. Precise export decodes and checks every source frame, preserving frame timestamps and copying supported original audio without re-encoding. It takes the time needed for detection and encoding. Compatibility export records during real-time playback when precise export is unavailable. Imported-video exports include a three-second animated MANLYFY logo, app name and tagline. Creator edition can disable the ending. Small or obscured faces may be missed.

Downloads & sharing

Photos download as watermark-free PNG files at their decoded resolution, up to 8192 pixels on the longest edge and 32 megapixels. 9:16 and square formats fit your whole photo with a dark border. Video export defaults to original dimensions up to a 3840-pixel longest edge. Choose 1080p or 720p for smaller files. Precise export checks available encoders and falls back to a supported size when necessary; the result shows the actual resolution. MP4/H.264 is preferred when supported; WebM is also available. Encoding still re-compresses the video; 4K is a resolution limit, not a lossless guarantee. Large exports stop at a 384 MB memory limit instead of presenting a partial clip as complete. Some social apps need a format conversion. Native file sharing appears where supported; otherwise download and share from your gallery.

Install & offline

On Android Chrome, tap Install app or use the browser menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen. After the tracker has loaded once online, previously loaded app and tracking files can be reused offline. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted.

A few honest limits

Recording requires MediaRecorder and canvas capture support. Record with the tab visible; switching away stops the recording. Clips stop at 3 minutes to protect phone memory. Tracking is a playful 2D effect, not a perfect 3D wrap. Browsers without WebGL2 use a local CPU worker. Precision mode scans at the next available frame and uses optical flow plus a coarse fast-motion recovery between scans, targeting up to 60 camera frames per second where supported. Fast turns, blur, covered faces, and leaving the frame can still interrupt it. Different designs follow short-lived spatial tracks, not biometric identities; long disappearances or occlusion can break the assignment. First face acquisition can take longer on slower devices. Real Android devices and low-powered hardware still need hands-on testing.
